# Project 7: Mock internal audit

[← Repository home](../../README.md)

## Objective

Audit the work from Projects 1 to 6 as an internal auditor: pick 10 controls, review evidence, record findings and severity, plan corrective actions and write an audit report.

## Deliverables

| File | What it contains |
|---|---|
| [Audit plan and checklist](audit-plan-and-checklist.md) | Plan, rating scale, seven cross-project checks and a 10-control checklist (Control, Evidence, Finding, Severity, Action Plan) |
| [Audit report](audit-report.md) | Conclusion, results, 10 findings, good practice, corrective action plan, lessons learned and next steps |

## Key results

- **10 findings:** 1 major nonconformity, 5 minor nonconformities and 4 observations (1 High, 7 Medium, 2 Low).
- Conclusion: **not yet ready for a Stage 1 audit**; the biggest gap is that the policy set is not approved.
- The audit **reconciled the portfolio against itself**: control counts, risk-to-control links, action IDs and vendor counts, finding two overdue actions on 10 Dec 2026.

## Improvements over the basic brief

- Added **cross-checks between documents**, so the audit tests the whole portfolio rather than ten rows in isolation.
- Findings use a **type and severity scale**, with every finding tied to an action ID, owner and due date.
- States the **independence limit** of auditing your own work, which a real audit would not accept.
- Turns the findings into **new actions** (ACT-25, ACT-26) that flow back into the dashboard.
